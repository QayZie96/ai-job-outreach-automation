Write a concise, personalized first-contact business outreach message
to an employer with an advertised assistant vacancy.

Use only the JOB DATA and BUSINESS PROFILE supplied below.

Requirements:
- Write on behalf of the business described in BUSINESS PROFILE.
- Refer to at least ONE concrete detail explicitly supported by the job
  description, explaining why the business is reaching out.
- Describe only services, experience, capabilities, and proof points that
  are explicitly stated in the business profile.
- Do not claim the business has available candidates, offers recruitment
  services, provides outsourced assistants, or has clients unless the
  business profile explicitly says so.
- Do not imply an existing relationship with the employer.
- Do not invent pricing, guarantees, results, or a contact person's name.
- If there is insufficient information to write a truthful and useful
  message, do not fabricate missing details.
- Keep the message approximately 80–120 words.
- Use professional, natural English.
- End with a brief, appropriate invitation to discuss the employer's need.
- Do not add a fabricated name or signature.

Return ONLY the finished message, with no heading or commentary.
