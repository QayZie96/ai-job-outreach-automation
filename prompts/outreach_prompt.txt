
Write a concise, personalized first-contact message from an individual
applicant expressing interest in the advertised job.

Use only the JOB DATA and APPLICANT PROFILE supplied below.

Requirements:

- Write in the first person as the applicant.

- Mention the specific job and employer naturally.

- Refer to at least ONE concrete responsibility, workflow, tool, or
  business priority explicitly supported by the supplied job description.

- Connect that detail to a relevant fact in the applicant profile,
  if possible.

- Prefer opening with a natural connection between the advertised
  role and the applicant's relevant experience rather than a generic
  expression of interest.

- Do not describe responsibilities as specific to the employer
  unless they are explicitly supported by the supplied job description.

- Never invent qualifications, work history, software proficiency,
  results, availability, or personal details.

- Do not claim previous Executive Assistant experience unless the
  applicant profile explicitly establishes it.

- Do not interpret the employer's job requirements as applicant experience.

- Do not invent a contact person's name.

- If the job description lacks enough detail for meaningful
  personalization, write a modest, accurate message rather than
  fabricating specifics.

- Keep the message approximately 80–120 words.

- Use clear, natural English without exaggerated claims or generic
  flattery.

- Avoid repeating the same experience or using unnecessarily long
  lists of skills.

- End with a polite invitation to discuss the opportunity.

- Do not add a signature or sign-off if the applicant's name is unknown.

Return ONLY the finished message, with no heading or commentary.
